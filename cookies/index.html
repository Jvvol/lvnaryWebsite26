This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies lvnary.com uses. Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, like browser local storage, work the same way.

What we use

This site does not set any cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising, or social media trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third parties.

The only thing we may store in your browser is your cookie choice, and only after you make one in Cookie settings:

Storage used by lvnary.com
NameCategoryPurposeTypeHow long
lvnary-consentStrictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choice so we don't ask againLocal storage, first partyUntil you clear it or our cookie policy changes

Categories

  • Strictly necessary: needed for the site to work or to remember your privacy choices. The item above is the only one we use.
  • Analytics: none in use.
  • Marketing: none in use.
  • Functional: none in use.

If we ever add something optional, we will list it here first, and it will not load until you agree to it.

Other sites

When you follow a link to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Amazon Music, Big Cartel, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook, those sites may set their own cookies. Their cookie policies apply there.

Your choices

Use at any time to see or change your choice. You can also clear stored data in your browser settings.

We honor Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends that signal, marketing stays off.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our use of cookies changes. If we add an optional category, we will ask for your choice again.
